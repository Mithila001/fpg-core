# Phase 2 observations

Run with Core 0.2.0 source, 29 Nov 2026. Synthetic specification: 200 x 200
project units, living/kitchen/dining/bedroom/hallway, 10-unit candidate grid, fixed
Optuna seed 314159. Old objective was the retained spatial-distribution evaluator;
new objective was spatial occupancy alone. Search score columns are objective
specific; compare occupancy metrics for physical outcome.

| Objective | Trials | Seconds | Mean search score | Mean occupancy | Best occupancy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Distribution | 100 | 1.257 | 13.08 | 88.91 | 100 |
| Occupancy | 100 | 1.368 | 90.27 | 90.27 | 100 |
| Distribution | 500 | 8.818 | 15.08 | 90.06 | 100 |
| Occupancy | 500 | 10.133 | 88.95 | 88.95 | 100 |

At these counts and on this small synthetic space, occupancy did not beat the
distribution objective on mean occupancy at 500 trials; it was marginally higher
at 100. Both found candidates with approximately 100 occupancy. Occupancy cost was
about 6.4% and 11.9% more time at 100 and 500. This does not establish improvement
on real prepared inputs; the old objective's low raw scores are not comparable.

Controlled evaluator maps ran through the circulation router and all five default
quality evaluators (the circulation, occupancy, clearance and bedroom group results
are saved in `component_results.json`). The assigned attached-bathroom relationship
scored 100 proximity at zero boundary distance and 0 at 86.98 units; the aggregate
circulation result fell from 81.93 to 74.44. Exterior clearance fell from 100 on the
well-arranged map to 73.16 when access was blocked. Bedroom compactness fell from
100 to 0 on the scattered map, and unrelated living/dining points were reported as
intrusions. An assigned attached bathroom placed on the group connection was not
reported as an intrusion. These are evaluator self-checks on hand-authored maps,
not architectural judgments.

The initial-generation solver profile returned INFEASIBLE for all three hand-authored
maps (desirable, clustered, scattered) under the synthetic specification. The
previous basic solver check, with constraints disabled, solved all three optimally;
mean center movement was 11.76, 21.67 and 10.69 units. The constrained result cannot
be used to compare candidate quality because this synthetic specification does not
satisfy the profile's full architectural assumptions. A prepared request and valid
candidate maps are still needed for the representative constrained-generation test.

Focused allocation/occupancy/center tests passed (4 tests); floor-plan solver tests
passed (121 tests). Lab's Core verifier and profile validation passed. The candidate
flow integration test fails before scoring when its random sampled map cannot route
a bathroom to a living room. Two existing floor-plan-scoring tests also fail on an
unrelated fixture that includes `kitchen` absent from its specification.

Not yet measured: component-level proximity/clearance/group maps, full P01 configs,
real prepared request comparisons, representative constrained solver generations,
or replay/export rendering. No recommendation to adjust configured weights or
distances follows from this synthetic sample.
