# E01 experiment family: floor traffic

E01A explores how preset trips occupy a final floor plan. Select one of four
hand-authored plans, then click **Simulate traffic**. The Paths view shows routes,
start circles, destination diamonds, and 8-unit-wide walking footprints; use the path checkboxes to select any combination
of routes. Select one view mode at a time in the right-hand panel. Heatmap shows accumulated preset-trip coverage. Zoom and
pan on the Plotly canvas. Room statistics include mean and peak traffic and the
percentage of floor untouched by any route.

Plans use the serialized Core `FloorPlan` shape and 10 project units per metre.
The fixtures are not generated or loaded from the database. P01C final plans can also be copied and pasted for the current session. Each has doors,
windows, two bedrooms and two bathrooms. Trips include veranda to living and
kitchen, bedrooms to the nearest reachable bathroom and kitchen, kitchen to dining,
and living to dining. Each route starts and ends at an interior point in the
room's largest walkable area, chosen for maximum clearance from its boundary.
Nearest means the shortest valid route between those points, with room ID
breaking ties. Each trip has equal weight.

The Lab-only experiment computes clearance-aware shortest paths with a visibility
graph and smooths their corners. Solid room walls and windows block travel; only
interior doors connect rooms. Fixture doors and the standard generated doors are 10 units wide. The entire 8-unit-wide footprint must clear walls.
Unreachable trips are reported and omitted from coverage. A 2-unit grid measures
the fraction of each cell covered by each trip. Zero is unused; higher values
indicate more overlapping trips. This is a geometric approximation, not a model of
people, furniture, schedules or observed use. Heatmap and room statistics describe
only the preset trips and do not recommend furniture or storage placement.

Results belong to the current page session and disappear on refresh or plan change.
No experiment history, configuration, or Core generation call is involved. The
quadratic visibility graph is intended for these small plans. Run the focused check
from `fpg-lab/` with `.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_e01a.py`.

Pasted plans preserve room identities and openings and are converted to 10 units per metre. Trips are derived from available room types; unavailable or inaccessible routes are reported.
